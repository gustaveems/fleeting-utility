# Fleeting Utility — Initial Technical & Gameplay Spec

## 1. Core Mechanics & Systems Architecture

### A. Focus & Chemical System (State Machine)
* **High-Focus State (Drugged):** High crafting speed, altered vision shaders (vibrant edge detection, high contrast), accelerated tool mastery.
* **The Crash State:** Slower movement, heavy chromatic aberration, rising "Sensory Overload" meter.
* **Self-Soothing Loop:** Mandatory interaction state to lower Sensory Overload when unmedicated.

### B. Workspace & Spatial Management
* **Room Degradation Matrix:** Tracks clutter, broken objects, paint splatters, and structural damage over time.
* **Crafting & Tool Mastery:** Minigames/interaction nodes for building, painting, and assembling tools within a tight leased room.

### C. Dreamscape System (Autumn Valley)
* **Trigger:** Initiated during crashes, forced detox, or sleep cycles.
* **Visual Style:** High-saturation autumn palette, expansive nature, 50-foot monolithic blood-marked statues.
* **Mechanic:** Exploration-focused, narrative audio logs/statue interactions uncovering the loss of human sacrifice and collective purpose.
