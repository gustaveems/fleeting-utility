# Fleeting Utility — Master World & Narrative Brief

## 1. Premise & Core Themes
* **Core Conflict:** Alienation under hyper-accelerationism, explored through a protagonist trapped in a non-stop cycle of manic creation and chemical self-soothing.
* **The Friction of Owned Space:** Every space, tool, and piece of infrastructure is leased and commodified. All creative output has a "fleeting utility" because nothing can abide or outlast the individual.
* **Key Visual Contrast:** The dark, claustrophobic decay of the protagonist's leased apartment workspace vs. the vibrant, ancient autumn valley and 50-foot blood-marked statues in his dreams.

## 2. Narrative Arc & Key Events
* **The Chemical Cycle:** To maintain high-tempo crafting and spatial mastery, the protagonist relies on an advanced focus-enhancing drug. As the drug wears off, cognitive overload forces him into mechanical self-soothing (compulsive masturbation, fixating on trivial objects).
* **Chemical Gentrification:** As the economy accelerates toward a breaking point, the ruling elite enclose the drug supply, making it an exclusive commodity for the top tier.
* **The Walk & The Betrayal:** The protagonist takes a walk through a simulated corporate park to feel pseudo-nature. Upon returning, he finds his sanctuary ransacked, his art destroyed, and his stash stolen. Looking out the window, he spots his friend wearing high-status luxury shoes—the marker of an accelerationist climber who sold him out.
* **The Hesitation:** He freezes at his door, realizing confrontation is pointless; ownership is an illusion and his work is gone.
* **Forced Detox & Dual Visions:** Stuck in his ruined workspace, physical withdrawal sets in. He experiences dual flashes cutting between his dark, rotting room and the warm, autumn valley. Deprived of his chemical ego shield, he sees the blood-marked statues not as terrifying sacrifices, but as monuments to a time when humans stood for something greater than atomic self-interest.
* **The Climax (Technological Interception):** As he fades out and feels at peace, an approaching corporate ambulance resuscitates him against his will. He wakes up violently in the ambulance, screaming in raw agony—fully unmedicated, lucid, and trapped back inside the system.
